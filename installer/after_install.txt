PIN IT TO YOUR TASKBAR

Windows does not let an installer pin anything to the taskbar - Microsoft
removed that in Windows 10 to stop installers filling it up. It takes one
moment by hand:

  1. Open HozaSend (the Finish page can do it for you).
  2. Right-click its icon in the taskbar while it is running.
  3. Choose "Pin to taskbar".

A desktop shortcut and a Start menu entry were created for you, if you asked
for them on the previous page.


THE FIREWALL

HozaSend finds other devices by broadcasting on your local network. If you did
not tick the firewall option - or you installed without administrator rights -
Windows will ask about it the first time you open the app.

Choose ALLOW, and make sure "Private networks" is ticked. If it is blocked,
HozaSend will run but other devices will never find this computer.


HOW IT WORKS

Open HozaSend on this PC and on your phone, both on the same Wi-Fi or the
phone's hotspot. They find each other automatically. No internet is needed at
any point, and nothing is ever uploaded anywhere.
