# Privacy Policy — HozaSend

**Effective date:** 31 August 2026
**Application:** HozaSend (Windows, Android)
**Publisher:** Rahoz Osman Salim ("we", "us")
**Contact:** [hozahoza2001@gmail.com](mailto:hozahoza2001@gmail.com)

## Summary

HozaSend does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. It has no
accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.
Files you send travel directly from one of your devices to the other over your
own local network, and never pass through us or any third party.

## Information we collect

**None.** We operate no servers and receive no data from the app. Nothing you do
in HozaSend is reported to us or to anyone else.

## Information the app handles on your device

All of the following stays on your own devices and on your own local network.

| Data | Why it exists | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Device name and a randomly generated device ID | So the other device can show you which device is offering to connect | Broadcast on your local network (LAN/Wi-Fi/hotspot) only |
| Local IP address and port | Needed to open the direct connection between the two devices | Your local network only |
| Six-digit pairing code | Shown on both screens so you can confirm the connection | Held in memory during pairing |
| Files you choose to send | The purpose of the app | Sent directly to the receiving device you approved. Received files are saved to your chosen folder (by default `Downloads/HozaSend`) |
| App settings (theme, notifications, auto-accept, download folder) | To remember your preferences | Local app storage |
| Transfer history (file names, sizes, dates) | So you can see what was sent or received | Local app storage. You can clear it in the app |

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, removes the settings and history.
Files you already received stay in your download folder, where they belong to
you.

## Network use

HozaSend uses your network only to find nearby devices (UDP beacons on the local
subnet) and to carry the transfer itself (a direct TCP connection between the two
devices). It works with no internet connection at all — a hotspot with nothing
behind it is a perfectly good network. The app does not contact any web service,
back end, or update server.

Because transfers stay on your local network, use HozaSend on networks you trust.
Every connection must be confirmed with a matching six-digit code on both
screens, every incoming transfer must be accepted, and every file is verified
with a SHA-256 checksum before it is given its final name.

## Permissions

**Windows:** local network access, so the app can discover and connect to your
other device; access to the folders you pick for sending and receiving.

**Android:** network and Wi-Fi state and multicast, to discover devices on the
local network; local network sockets, to carry the transfer; notifications, to
show transfer progress and completion; foreground service and wake lock, to keep
a transfer running while the screen is off; storage, to read the files you pick
and save the files you receive.

None of these permissions are used to collect information about you.

## Third parties

HozaSend contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. It
does not share data with third parties, because it does not collect any.

If you installed the app from the Microsoft Store or another app store, that
store may collect its own data about the download under its own privacy policy.
That is between you and the store; we receive nothing from it.

## Children's privacy

HozaSend is not directed at children and collects no personal information from
anyone, including children under 13.

## Your rights

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct,
export, or delete on your behalf. The data on your device is under your control
at all times, and clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes it.
